Business sites

For longarm quilters and small quilting businesses: a fast public website, a quote-request form, estimates the customer signs online, and a project queue.

What a business site is

A business site is a QuiltHosting account set up as a business rather than a guild. It has customers instead of members, no member limit, and its own server-rendered website with a business-style page builder. QuiltHosting staff switch an account to the business type on request; it cannot be changed from the account itself.

Business sites are served on the free subdomain or a custom domain (see Domains). The built-in quilthosting.com/g/… address shows the guild-style site instead, so share the subdomain or your own domain.

The business admin

The sidebar shows Website (pages), Appearance, Domain & Launch, Business details, Projects and Quilting rates, plus the shared screens (Customers, Events, Store, Payments, Email, Forms, Team, Settings). The page builder works as described in Website builder, with business sections such as Service cards, Testimonials, Contact form and Quote request. Business accounts start with the same five starter pages as a guild (including two "Become a member" sections you will probably remove).

The dashboard's setup checklist is written for guilds. On a business account the steps about a guild logo, membership levels, dues payouts and first member do not apply and may never show as done; use it as a rough guide only.

Business details

Name, phone, email, street, city, state and ZIP. These appear in the site footer and are published as structured data so search engines can show your business correctly. The same screen has the site Logo and the Site menu editor. The email address here receives quote-request notifications.

Appearance

Four presets — Berry (default), Ocean, Forest, Charcoal — heading and body fonts, and an Advanced section for every colour. A contrast hint warns when text on the page background would be hard to read (it needs at least 4.5 to 1). You can switch off the "Powered by QuiltHosting" footer line.

Launch: "Site is live to the public"

While QuiltHosting is in private preview, every site on the platform sits behind an access password. The Site is live to the public switch on Domain & Launch lifts that password for your site on your subdomain or custom domain, so customers can visit without it. Until you switch it on, visitors to your domain see the "Private preview" password page.

  • Only your public pages, images, sitemap and quote pages are opened. The admin, the portal and the docs stay behind the password even on your domain.
  • The switch only exists for business accounts. Guild sites stay behind the platform password until QuiltHosting launches publicly.

Quotes and projects

A project starts when a customer fills in the Quote request section on your site (project type: longarm quilting, custom quilt or T-shirt quilt). The customer sees a ballpark figure on screen based on your rates, gets an acknowledgement email, and you get a notification. They can attach up to 5 photos (10 MB each) while the request is new.

Admin → Projects lists requests with a reference like ABC-0042 (the prefix is set on the Quilting rates screen) and a status filter. Open one to see the request, photos, and line items.

StatusMeaning / what you do
SubmittedNew request. Edit the line items (service, add-on or discount; description, quantity, unit price) and click Save lines.
EstimatedSend estimate to customer emails a private link (valid 90 days). At least one line item is required. You can revise and send again; Resend link issues a fresh link and cancels the old one.
SignedThe customer approved the estimate online. Both of you receive a copy of the agreement.
In progress, CompletedMove the project along with Update status as you work.
Declined, CancelledClosed. Declined is set by you (there is no customer decline button); cancelled can be set from any open status.

Totals are always recalculated from the line items on the server; the customer's request is matched to (or creates) a customer record only when you send the estimate, so a spam form cannot fill your customer list.

Quilting rates and agreement

Admin → Quilting rates & agreement. Rates are entered per 100 square inches for quilting and batting, per inch for binding, and as flat fees for thread, backing preparation, custom design and T-shirt finishing, plus per-block for T-shirt quilts; three minimum charges; a rush percentage; a reference prefix; and the agreement title and text the customer signs. Leave a rate blank to turn that service off. If a request needs a rate you have not set, or leaves out dimensions, no price is shown rather than a wrong one.

What the customer sees

The estimate link opens on your site: the line items, your agreement text, a box to type their name, and a consent checkbox. Signing records the exact text they saw and emails them a copy with a fingerprint of that text. The same link then shows the signed copy with a Print / Save PDF button, and keeps working after the link's 90 days. The page is private, not indexed, and never cached.

What is not included yet

  • Payments and invoices for projects: the project ends at Completed; take payment however you do today.
  • Configurable service types with their own fields and pricing rules: currently the three project types and the fixed rate list above.
  • Reminders or follow-up emails beyond the acknowledgement, estimate and signed-copy emails.
  • Projects are for owners and admins only.
