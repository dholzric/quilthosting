Member portal

Where members renew, see events and receipts, find each other, and manage their own details — without a password.

Signing in

Members open quilthosting.com/portal?slug=your-guild (or the Member portal link on your site), type their email, and click Email me a login link. The screen always says "Check your email for a login link", whether or not the address is known. The email contains a link that:

  • is valid for 15 minutes;
  • works once — a second click shows "This sign-in link has expired or was already used", with a link back to the portal to request another;
  • signs the member in for 7 days on that browser.

Members can also use Sign in with Google if their Google account uses the same email. A person who belongs to several guilds is shown a picker; if the portal does not know which guild, it asks for the guild's web address. Requests for links are limited to 10 per 10 minutes from one connection.

While QuiltHosting is in private preview, the whole site is behind an access password. A member who opens a sign-in link on a phone that has not entered that password is asked for it first, and then lands signed in. The link is used up before the password screen, so a member who gives up at that point needs a new link.

Renewing, auto-renew and cards

  • Renew membership uses the member's current level (or lets them pick one). A free level activates at once; a paid level opens Stripe Checkout — as a subscription when the level is set to auto-renew, otherwise as a one-off payment.
  • The Profile tab shows the card on file (brand, last four digits, expiry) when the member has an auto-renew subscription. Update payment card opens Stripe's secure page to change it.
  • Stop automatic renewal cancels the subscription; the membership stays active until its end date.

Receipts and documents

Opening a receipt or downloading a document creates a private link that is valid for 10 minutes and only for that one item, so a copied link cannot be used later or by someone else. Receipts open as a printable page with Print / Save as PDF. Documents are the files officers uploaded under Admin → Documents; members with a cancelled membership cannot download them.

Directory

The directory lists active members who have Show me in the member directory ticked (it is ticked by default). It shows name, photo, member-since date, and the bio, headline and interests from their profile. Email addresses and phone numbers are never shown. Unticking the box removes the member and their photo entirely; there is no per-field control.

Forum

Any member who is not cancelled can start a topic (title up to 200 characters, body up to 10,000) and reply. Members cannot edit or delete their own posts. Officers moderate under Admin → Forum: pin, unpin or delete topics.

Guild info pages

Pages you publish in the website builder appear under Guild Info, including those marked Members only (labelled "(members only)"). Members-only pages never appear on the public site.

Profile and email preferences

  • Members can change their first and last name, phone, bio (up to 2,000 characters), headline, interests and website, upload a photo (up to 5 MB), and fill in your guild's custom fields. Email cannot be changed by the member; an officer changes it in the admin.
  • Send me newsletters and announcements controls newsletters and automations from your guild. Receipts, renewal reminders, sign-in links and event confirmations are always sent.

Sessions

A sign-in lasts 7 days on that browser. After that, or after Sign out, the member requests a new link. If a page shows "Your session expired. Please sign in again.", that is all that happened.

Sharing the link

https://quilthosting.com/portal?slug=your-slug

Put it in newsletters and on your website. On your own subdomain or custom domain, /portal works too. Pair it with the public join page for people who are not members yet (see Domains for your site addresses).
