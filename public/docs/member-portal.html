Member portal

Self-service for members: renewals, events, documents, directory, and more.

Sign-in (passwordless)

Members open /portal?slug=your-guild and enter email. QuiltHosting emails a magic link (or they can use Google if enabled). No guild-managed passwords.

Magic links expire. If a link fails, request a new one from the portal login screen.

ProfileName, phone, bio, photo, directory visibility, custom fields

Renew, cancel auto-renew, update card

  • Renew — starts Checkout (one-time or subscription depending on level)
  • Cancel auto-renew — stops the Stripe subscription; membership stays active until end date
  • Update payment card — opens Stripe Customer Portal for the card on file

Directory, bio & photo

On Profile, members can:

  • Upload a photo (shown in the member directory)
  • Add bio, headline, and interests
  • Opt out of the directory entirely

If the guild enables a public directory, opted-in members also appear on the public guild page.

Forum

Any non-cancelled member can post topics and replies. Officers moderate (pin/delete) under Admin → Forum.

Share the link
