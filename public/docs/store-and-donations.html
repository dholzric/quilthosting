Store & donations

Sell raffle tickets, patterns, pins and show merchandise from your site; accept donations; and put join, events and store widgets on a WordPress site you already have.

Products

Admin → Store. A product has a name, description, price in dollars, an optional SKU, an optional inventory count (leave blank for unlimited), and a taxable switch. There are no product photos, sizes or variants, and no digital downloads. Deleting a product deactivates it; sold-out and inactive products are hidden from the site.

Cart and checkout

On the public site visitors add items to a cart (up to 20 of each item, 20 different items), see subtotal, tax and total, enter their email and pay through Stripe Checkout. A product priced at 0 is ordered without a card. Money goes to your connected Stripe account; the buyer receives a receipt email; the payment appears in Admin → Payments with type "store".

Stock held during checkout

When a cart is checked out, the items are taken out of stock immediately and held for 30 minutes — the time the Stripe page stays open. If any item has run out, the checkout is refused and the cart says which item. If the buyer does not pay, the stock is put back as soon as Stripe reports the checkout expired, or by a sweep that runs every minute and clears holds 10 minutes after they lapse. Two people can never buy the same last unit.

Sales tax

Store → Tax settings: a single Sales tax % (type 7 for 7%, or 7.25 for 7.25%; 25% is the ceiling) and a label (default "Sales tax"). Tax is charged on taxable products in the cart and shown as its own line. The single-product Buy button and the store embed do not add tax. Dues, event tickets and donations are never taxed. QuiltHosting does not calculate tax by location.

Orders

There is no orders screen. What was bought, by whom and for how much is visible in Admin → Payments (type "store", with the buyer's email). QuiltHosting does not collect a shipping address or track shipping; arrange pickup or ask for an address in the product description.

Donations

Turn on Accept donations in Settings → Public profile. The public site then shows a donation block with $10, $25 and $50 buttons and an Other amount (any amount from $1 to $10,000). Donations are one-time; there are no recurring donations. The donor gets a receipt email, "Receipt: Donation to (guild)"; if the email belongs to a member, the payment is attached to their record. Year-end reports list donations separately.

Guild logo

Settings → Public profile → Guild logo. PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 2 MB; SVG is not accepted. The file's contents are checked, not just its name. The logo appears next to your guild name on your site and on the join embed. Wide wordmarks and square marks both work; transparent PNG looks best.

Embeds for WordPress and other sites

Settings → Embed widgets shows three snippets to paste into any page (a WordPress "Custom HTML" block works):

<iframe src="https://quilthosting.com/embed/YOUR-SLUG/join"
  width="100%" height="520" style="border:0;max-width:440px"
  loading="lazy"></iframe>
WidgetAddressWhat it does
Join/embed/YOUR-SLUG/joinName, email, level and your join-form custom fields; sends the visitor to checkout for paid levels.
Events/embed/YOUR-SLUG/eventsLists upcoming events; the Register button opens your QuiltHosting site in a new tab.
Store/embed/YOUR-SLUG/storeOne-item Buy buttons with an email field; no cart and no sales tax.

Each widget has a small "Powered by QuiltHosting" line linking to your site. While QuiltHosting is in private preview the embeds are behind the same access password as everything else, so they will only display for people who have entered it.
