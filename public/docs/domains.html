Domains

Every guild has a built-in address and a free subdomain. When you are ready, point a domain you own at your site.

Your three addresses

AddressExampleNotes
Built-inquilthosting.com/g/your-slugAlways works.
Free subdomainyour-slug.quilthosting.comSet up automatically when the guild is created.
Custom domainwww.yourguild.orgOptional; you own the domain.

All three show the same site, and /portal and /admin work on each of them. The dashboard shows which one is your current public address.

The built-in address

The slug is the web address you chose when creating the guild: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, at least two characters, unique across QuiltHosting. It cannot be changed afterwards. On your subdomain or custom domain the /g/your-slug part is not needed — /g/… links simply forward to the shorter form.

Your free subdomain

When a guild is created, QuiltHosting sets up your-slug.quilthosting.com in the background. The dashboard shows a badge:

BadgeMeaning
subdomain liveWorking; this is your public address.
subdomain pendingStill being set up. Click Check again.
subdomain failedSetup was refused; the reason is shown. Click Retry. If it keeps failing, contact support.
subdomain not set upSetup was skipped. Click Check again.
no badgeGuilds created before this feature existed have no recorded status. Click Check again once to fill it in.

Setup takes effect as soon as it succeeds; there is no waiting period. Owners and admins can use Check again and Retry. A slug of www or api cannot be used as a subdomain.

A domain you own

Admin → Settings → Custom domain (owners and admins). Type your domain — www.yourguild.org or yourguild.org; either way both the bare domain and www are set up — and click Save domain. Then add these records at your domain registrar or DNS host:

TypeNameValueNotes
CNAMEwwwcustomers.quilthosting.comRecommended. Points www.yourguild.org at QuiltHosting.
CNAME or ALIAS@customers.quilthosting.comThe bare domain needs CNAME flattening (Cloudflare DNS) or an ALIAS/ANAME record at your DNS host. Or redirect the bare domain to www.
  • The card shows the status of each hostname and its certificate, for example www: active / SSL active. Use Refresh SSL status to check again after changing DNS. DNS changes can take a while to spread.
  • You cannot use quilthosting.com itself or another guild's subdomain, and a domain already used by another guild is refused.
  • Both www and the bare domain serve the site; QuiltHosting does not redirect one to the other.
  • Business sites set their domain on the Domain & Launch screen instead, which shows the same records.

HTTPS

Certificates are issued automatically for your subdomain and your custom domain (first 100 hostnames are free on the platform). Nothing to install. The site requires TLS 1.2 or newer.

Removing a custom domain

Clear custom domain removes the hostnames from QuiltHosting; your free subdomain keeps working ("Remove the custom domain? Free subdomain still works."). Remember to remove or repoint the DNS records at your registrar.
