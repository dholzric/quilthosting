Email & automations

Newsletters and announcements to your members, scheduled sends, delivery tracking, unsubscribe handling, welcome sequences, and optional text messages.

Audiences

Admin → Email. The Send to dropdown offers:

AudienceWho is included
ActiveMembers with status Active
PendingMembers with status Pending
LapsedMembers with status Lapsed
Everyone (except cancelled)All members other than Cancelled
A membership levelMembers with a current active membership at that level
A groupMembers in that group (except cancelled)

Under the dropdown the screen shows the recipient count and how many people are opted out and therefore not included — anyone who unsubscribed, turned newsletters off in the portal, or whose address is blocked after a bounce or spam complaint. There is no ad-hoc list of typed-in addresses; create a group instead. Groups are created and managed on this screen.

Composing

  • Subject, Send to, Layout (Plain, Newsletter or Announcement), a Quick start template (monthly newsletter, event invitation, renewal nudge, welcome note), the Message, and an optional Schedule time.
  • The message is plain text; each line becomes a paragraph. Tick Message is raw HTML if you paste HTML from another tool. There is no rich-text editor and no test send; View on a sent email shows what went out.
  • Emails come from the QuiltHosting sending address with your guild's name in the layout. Replies do not reach your guild — put your contact email in the message when you want answers.

Merge tags

Type these in the subject or message and they are replaced for each recipient:

TagBecomes
{{first_name}}The member's first name, or "there" if it is blank
{{last_name}}Last name
{{email}}Their email address
{{guild_name}}Your guild's name
{{level_name}}Their membership level — filled in only when the audience is a membership level
{{end_date}}Their renewal date, like "December 31, 2026" — only for a level audience
{{unsubscribe_url}}The recipient's personal unsubscribe link

Tags are not case-sensitive and single braces work too. Unknown tags become empty. If you do not place {{unsubscribe_url}} yourself, an unsubscribe line is added at the bottom of every newsletter automatically.

Scheduling

Set a Schedule date and time to send later. The time is read in your browser's time zone. A scheduler runs every minute, so a scheduled send starts within about a minute of its time. Scheduled emails show in the list with a Cancel button until they start.

Sends to 75 or fewer people go out immediately while you wait. Larger sends are queued and worked through in the background at roughly 200 emails a minute, with the count updating in the list.

Delivery and statuses

Each newsletter shows sent ÷ recipients, plus failed and skipped counts (skipped means the address was opted out or blocked at the moment of sending). The Recent emails table lists individual messages with their delivery status:

StatusMeaning
acceptedHanded to the email provider
deliveredThe recipient's mail server accepted it
delayedThe provider is retrying delivery
bouncedThe address rejected it permanently (see below)
complainedThe recipient marked it as spam
failedCould not be sent after three attempts

Opens are counted with a tracking image and shown in the table; clicks are tracked through QuiltHosting links but are not yet shown in the admin. Delivered, delayed, bounced and complained statuses depend on delivery notices from the email provider being switched on for the platform.

Partial and Retry failed

A queued send finishes as sent when every recipient was accepted, or partial when at least one recipient still failed after three tries. A partial send shows a Retry failed button, which re-sends only to the recipients that failed and updates the counts. If the email provider is down for a whole batch, nothing is recorded and the same recipients are tried again a minute later, so nobody gets two copies.

Unsubscribe

  • Every newsletter and automation email carries an unsubscribe link and the headers that let Gmail, Apple Mail and others show a one-click Unsubscribe button.
  • Clicking the link opens a confirmation page; the person is only unsubscribed when they confirm (or when their mail client uses one-click). Old links keep working; they do not expire.
  • Unsubscribing turns off newsletters and automations for that person in your guild. Receipts, sign-in links, renewal reminders and event emails are still sent.
  • Members can turn newsletters back on with the Send me newsletters and announcements switch on their portal Profile. Note: a member who used the unsubscribe link stays excluded from newsletters even after re-ticking that switch; contact QuiltHosting support to clear it.

Bounces and complaints

  • A hard bounce (the address does not exist or refuses mail) blocks all email to that address across QuiltHosting, including sign-in links and receipts.
  • A spam complaint stops newsletters and automations from your guild to that person, but transactional emails continue.
  • These blocks are automatic; there is no admin screen to view or clear them. Contact support if an address was blocked in error.
  • A CSV import never removes an opt-out: if a person unsubscribed and is later removed and imported again, they stay unsubscribed.

One automatic email does respect opt-outs: the "we miss you" note sent 7 days after a member lapses. Dues recorded by hand, auto-renew charges and invoices send no email.

Automations

Admin → Automations. An automation is a sequence of emails sent after a member's membership is activated (by joining online, renewing, or paying dues through checkout). That is the only trigger available.

  • Each step has a delay in days (0–365), a subject and an HTML body; up to 12 steps. Steps are entered as a small list in the editor, with an example to copy.
  • Steps are sent by the daily job at 08:00 UTC, so a "day 0" step arrives the next morning rather than instantly.
  • Automation emails respect opt-outs and carry an unsubscribe link. Merge tags: {{first_name}}, {{last_name}}, {{email}}, {{guild_name}}.
  • Pause stops the sequence and cancels members part-way through; Activate starts it again for new activations. A member is not enrolled twice.

SMS (optional)

Admin → SMS. Enter your own Twilio account SID, auth token and sending number; they are stored in your guild's settings. Then send a one-off text (up to 1,600 characters) to a member with a phone number, and see a log of sent and failed messages. There is no SMS newsletter, no automatic text reminders, and no opt-in tracking — make sure you have permission to text people. Without Twilio details, messages are only logged as "queued" and are never sent.
