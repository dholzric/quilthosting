Billing & payments

How guild dues, platform fees, refunds, and invoices work.

Two money flows

FlowWho paysWhere money goes
Member payments Members (dues, events, store, donations) Guild’s connected Stripe account (destination charges)
Platform billing Guild → QuiltHosting QuiltHosting (Guild plan $24/mo after trial)

Stripe Connect

Admin → Settings → Billing & payouts → complete Express onboarding. Required for real payouts to the guild bank. Optional platform application fee (basis points) may apply if configured by QuiltHosting.

Platform plan (QuiltHosting fee)

  • Free: ≤ 30 active members
  • Trial: 30 days of Guild features
  • Guild: $24/month unlimited actives — upgrade Checkout from billing card

Manage or cancel platform subscription via Stripe Customer Portal from the same billing card.

Dues & auto-renew

  • Levels with renewal_type: auto create Stripe subscriptions at checkout
  • invoice.paid webhooks extend membership end dates
  • Portal: renew, cancel auto-renew, update card
  • Daily job: renewal reminders at 30 / 14 / 7 / 1 days; auto-lapse expired; win-back at 7 days lapsed
Test auto-renew thoroughly in Stripe test mode before relying on it for a live guild. See internal checklist docs/auto-renew-e2e.md in the repo.

Refunds

Admin → Payments → refund. One-click in-app refunds (a known Wild Apricot gap). Refunds go through Stripe to the original payment method when possible.

Receipts & QuickBooks

  • Members: printable receipts from portal invoices list
  • Donation receipt emails on successful donations
  • Admin: CSV exports; QuickBooks Desktop IIF via payments export

Numbered invoices

Admin → Invoices for multi-line bills (booth fees, sponsorships, etc.). Sequential numbers per guild. Print to PDF from the browser. Statuses: draft, sent, paid.
