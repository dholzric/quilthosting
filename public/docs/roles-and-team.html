Roles & team

Who can do what in the admin, how to add officers, and how to hand the guild to the next president.

The five roles

RoleIntended for
OwnerThe person ultimately responsible. Full access, plus the only role that can make or unmake other owners and cancel the platform subscription.
AdminOfficers who run the whole guild. Full access to everything except owner-only actions.
Membership chairMembers, levels, groups, dues, refunds, invoices, email and SMS, forms. Can view everything else.
Events chairEvents, galleries and forms. Can view most other areas.
ViewerLooks but cannot change anything.

Every request to the server is checked against this table, not just the buttons on screen. Roles belong to one guild; a person can hold different roles in different guilds.

Cancelling the platform subscription is owner-only. Platform staff at QuiltHosting can act with owner-level access on any guild for support.

Adding an officer

Admin → Team → enter their email and choose a role. They are added to the team immediately and receive an email, "You've been added to (guild) on QuiltHosting", telling them to sign in with Google using that address. There is no invitation to accept and nothing expires; if you typed the wrong address, remove that row. Owners and admins can add people; only an owner can grant the Owner role.

Changing roles and removing people

  • Owners and admins can change roles and remove people.
  • Only an owner can change an owner's role, promote someone to owner, or remove an owner.
  • The last owner cannot be removed ("Cannot remove the last owner").
  • Removing someone takes effect right away for new requests; a session they already have keeps working until it expires (up to 7 days). Remove departed officers promptly.

Handing over ownership

There is no separate "transfer ownership" button. When a new president takes over:

  1. The current owner adds the new person to the team (any role).
  2. The current owner changes the new person's role to Owner. A guild can have more than one owner.
  3. The outgoing owner is then removed, or changes their own role, by either owner.

Do the same for Stripe: the Stripe Express account belongs to the guild, so update its contact details in the Stripe dashboard rather than creating a new one.

What read-only looks like

Screens a role cannot see at all are missing from the sidebar. Screens a role can view but not change show a banner, "Read-only for your role — you can view this screen but not change it.", and the buttons that would make changes are greyed out. In the website builder the status pill reads Read-only; you can still browse pages and use Preview. If a change is attempted anyway, the server refuses it.
