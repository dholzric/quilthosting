Feature reference

What QuiltHosting does today (version 0.56, private preview), and what it deliberately does not do yet.

Membership

  • Membership levels with price, term in months, and manual or automatic (card-on-file) renewal; levels are archived, never deleted
  • Members with statuses pending / active / lapsed / cancelled, notes, custom fields (text or dropdown), groups, directory opt-out
  • Dues recorded by hand (cash, check, comp) with membership history
  • Renewal reminders 30/14/7/1 days out, automatic lapsing, a one-time win-back email
  • CSV import with Wild Apricot heading matching, preview before writing, per-row error reports; CSV export
  • Free plan up to 30 active members; 30-day Guild trial; Guild plan $24/month
  • Chapters: child guilds for multi-chapter organisations

Events

  • Public events with member and non-member pricing, capacity, waitlist (manual promotion), up to 20 text/dropdown questions
  • Seats held atomically during a 30-minute checkout; payments never applied twice
  • Repeating events (weekly, monthly by date, monthly by nth weekday; up to 60 occurrences)
  • Volunteer slots with public sign-up
  • Ticket codes, attendee list, check-in that works offline, CSV export
  • Per-event calendar files, a subscribable calendar feed, month calendar views, reminders 7 and 1 day before
  • Photo galleries (public; members-only flag reserved for later)

Communications

  • Newsletters to status, level or group audiences with opted-out counts; three layouts; merge tags; scheduled sends that start within a minute
  • Per-recipient delivery status, open counts, partial-send retry
  • Unsubscribe links, one-click unsubscribe headers, bounce and complaint suppression, portal newsletter switch
  • Automations: email sequences triggered by membership activation, up to 12 steps
  • One-to-one SMS through your own Twilio account
  • Forms with six field types, conditional fields, and a contact-form page section

Website

  • Starter site with five pages for every new guild
  • Section-based page builder: hero, heading, rich text, image, gallery, FAQ, button, join call-to-action, events list, store list, divider, spacer, embed; business sites add service cards, testimonials, contact form and quote request
  • Autosaved drafts, preview at desktop/tablet/mobile through the live renderer, publish, discard, 50-version history with restore-into-draft, conflict detection, trash
  • Page settings: address, menu, members-only, search title/description, hide from search engines; redirects when an address changes
  • Site menu editor; guild themes (colour, style, font); business appearance presets with contrast check
  • Blog posts on the guild site
  • Members-only pages and documents in the portal; member forum; member directory
  • Built-in address, free subdomain, custom domains with automatic certificates
  • Join, events and store widgets for WordPress and other sites

Commerce

  • Stripe Connect Express payouts to the guild's own bank; platform fee 0% by default
  • Store products with SKU, inventory and taxable flag; multi-item cart with a flat sales-tax rate; stock reserved during checkout
  • One-time donations ($1–$10,000) with receipts
  • Full refunds from the admin
  • Receipts (printable, and emailed for store and donations); numbered invoices with print-to-PDF and mark-paid
  • Treasurer reports: annual summary by year and a printable per-member statement / donation receipt
  • Payments CSV, QuickBooks Desktop IIF export, QuickBooks Online push

Business sites

  • Server-rendered public site with appearance presets, business details and structured data
  • Quote-request form with ballpark pricing from your rate card and photo uploads
  • Project queue: submitted → estimated → signed → in progress → completed, with line items and a customer-signed agreement
  • Per-site launch switch that lifts the private-preview password for that site

Integrations and admin

  • Five roles enforced on every request; read-only mode in the admin
  • Google sign-in for officers; one-time email links for members; 7-day sessions
  • Public API with scoped keys, member create/update with idempotency keys, and REST hooks
  • Outbound webhooks for Zapier and Make: seven events, signed payloads, retries, replay, auto-disable
  • Admin and portal usable on phones as web apps; offline event check-in

Not included (yet)

  • Bulk edits on the members list (use CSV import)
  • Family or household memberships; calendar-year or fixed-date renewals
  • Online payment of invoices, invoice emails or reminders; recurring donations; partial refunds
  • Product photos, sizes or variants; an orders screen; shipping addresses
  • Members-only or draft events; automatic waitlist promotion; event cancellation emails; QR-code check-in
  • A rich-text email editor, test sends, or a screen for blocked addresses; SMS newsletters
  • Automations triggered by anything other than membership activation
  • Configurable service types and payments for business projects
  • Native app-store apps (the admin and portal are web apps)
