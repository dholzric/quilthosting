Getting started

From nothing to a guild with a website, a membership level and a way to get paid, in about an hour.

Signing in

  • Officers sign in at /admin with Google. Use the Google account whose email you want on the guild's records; there is no password to set.
  • Members never need the admin. They sign in to the member portal with a link emailed to them (valid 15 minutes, works once).

While QuiltHosting is in private preview you will be asked for the shared access password first.

Create your guild

After signing in, choose Create guild and fill in:

FieldNotes
Guild nameRequired. Shown everywhere.
Web addressRequired. Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, at least two characters, unique across QuiltHosting. Anything else you type is dropped. It cannot be changed later, so choose carefully: it becomes quilthosting.com/g/your-address and your-address.quilthosting.com.
City / StateOptional, up to 120 characters. Used on your starter pages.
Meeting scheduleOptional, up to 300 characters, e.g. "Second Tuesday, 7 pm, Grace Church hall". Used on your starter pages.

You become the guild's Owner. The guild starts on a 30-day Guild trial (see Trial and plan).

Your starter site

Every new guild gets a working website with five published pages — Home, About, Why Join, Meetings and Contact — in the classic look with the QuiltHosting rust colour. Every paragraph starts with "Sample text — replace me:" so you can find what to rewrite. Home and Why Join include a "Become a member" call-to-action that points at your membership levels. Edit the pages in Admin → Website; see Website builder.

The setup checklist

The dashboard shows a card, Get your guild ready, with "n of m done". Nothing is ticked by hand: each step is recomputed from your real data every time you open the dashboard, so it can never be out of date.

StepCounts as done when
Starter website createdYour guild has at least one page (true from the start).
Replace the sample text on your pagesNo page still contains "Sample text — replace me:".
Upload your guild logoA logo has been uploaded under Settings → Public profile.
Add a membership levelAt least one active level exists.
Connect Stripe for dues payoutsStripe Connect has been started, or all your levels are free.
Add or import your first memberAt least one member exists.
Invite another officer to help (optional)The team has two or more people.
Your web address (optional)Your free subdomain is live or a custom domain is set.

Each undone step has a do this link. The guild is "ready for members" once it has a level and payments are sorted; the optional steps never block that. Dismiss checklist shrinks it to one line ("Setup checklist · View your site"); any officer can dismiss it and reopen it from the same place.

Your public address

Your site is at quilthosting.com/g/your-address from the first minute. QuiltHosting also sets up your-address.quilthosting.com in the background; the dashboard shows a badge (subdomain live, pending, failed or not set up) and a Check again (or Retry) button. Guilds created before this feature show no badge until you click Check again once. Later, you can point a domain you own at the site. Details: Domains.

First membership level

Admin → Levels → add a level: a name, a price in dollars (type 25 or 25.00 for $25.00; 0 = free), a duration in months (12 for a year), and whether it renews manually or automatically by card. Add one level now; you can add Family, Lifetime or Student later. Full detail: Members & levels.

Connect payments

If any level, event or product costs money, go to Settings → Billing & payouts → Connect bank account. Stripe walks the guild through a short form (legal name, tax ID or SSN of the responsible person, bank account). Until this is finished, card payments still work but land on QuiltHosting's platform account for testing only. See Billing & payments.

Add your members

Members → Import CSV takes a spreadsheet export from Wild Apricot or anywhere else; column headings are matched for you and nothing is written until you confirm the preview. Or add members one at a time. Then share the portal link so they can sign in. Guide: CSV import.

Invite officers

Admin → Team → enter an email and a role (Admin, Membership chair, Events chair, Viewer, or Owner). They are added immediately and emailed; they sign in with Google using that address. See Roles & team.
