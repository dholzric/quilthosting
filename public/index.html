Questions guilds ask us

Can we move over from Wild Apricot?

Yes — export your contacts from Wild Apricot as CSV and import them here. Names, emails, phones, statuses, and notes map automatically. Most guilds are done in an afternoon, and we'll help if you get stuck.

How do payments work?

Dues, event fees, and donations are paid by card through Stripe, the same processor used by millions of businesses. Money goes to your guild's account, and you can issue refunds yourself with one click.

Our members aren't tech-savvy. Will they manage?

That's exactly who we design for. Members never create a password — they click a sign-in link sent to their email. Joining and registering for events is a single short form.

Who owns our member data?

Your guild does. Export your full member list and payment history as CSV any time, no questions asked.

What counts as an "active member" for pricing?

Only members with a current membership. Lapsed members, cancelled memberships, guests, and event attendees don't count toward your plan — ever.

Switching from Wild Apricot?
