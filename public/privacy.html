Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026 · Early access / private preview

This Privacy Policy explains how QuiltMap LLC (“we”, “us”, “QuiltMap”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use QuiltHosting at quilthosting.com and related services (the “Service”). QuiltHosting is a product of QuiltMap LLC, the parent company.

1. Who is responsible

QuiltMap LLC operates QuiltHosting. For platform accounts (guild admins), we are the controller of account data. For member and contact records a guild stores in the Service, the guild is typically the controller and we act as a processor / service provider on their behalf.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, authentication data (Google sign-in or magic links), role on a guild.
  • Guild content: member profiles, membership levels, events, registrations, pages, uploaded files, email blasts, custom fields, and notes that admins enter.
  • Payment data: amounts, statuses, and Stripe identifiers. Card numbers are handled by Stripe — we do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical data: IP address, user agent, request logs, cookies needed for site access (private preview gate) and sessions.

3. How we use information

  • Provide membership, events, email, and billing features
  • Process payments and Connect payouts via Stripe
  • Send transactional email (magic links, renewals, event confirmations, blasts you author)
  • Secure the Service, prevent abuse, and debug issues
  • Improve the product (aggregate, non-identifying metrics where possible)

We do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

We share data with infrastructure and product vendors only as needed to run the Service:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, database (D1), storage (R2), edge compute
  • Stripe — payments, Connect onboarding, refunds, subscriptions
  • Resend — outbound email delivery
  • Google — optional OAuth sign-in

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Cookies

We use a signed cookie for the private preview site gate and may use local storage in the browser for admin/portal session tokens. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Retention

We retain account and guild data while the guild is active. After deletion or long inactivity we may purge data after a reasonable period. Backups may persist for a limited time. Payment records may be kept longer for accounting and fraud prevention.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and vendor security practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Report concerns to hello@quilthosting.com.

8. Your choices

  • Guild admins can export and edit member data in the admin UI.
  • Members can update profile fields in the portal where enabled.
  • Request account deletion by emailing us from your registered address.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit children’s personal information without appropriate parental consent and legal basis.

10. International

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from elsewhere, you understand information may be processed in the U.S. and other countries where our vendors operate.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised Policy.
