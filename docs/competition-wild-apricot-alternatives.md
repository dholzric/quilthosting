## Why Organizations Look for Wild Apricot Alternatives

Wild Apricot launched in 2006 and built a loyal base in North American associations and clubs. But since its acquisition by Personify Corp in September 2017, a clear pattern has emerged in customer feedback and sales calls with organizations evaluating a switch.

> "It's failing. It's not a very easy to use system anymore… they were purchased by a company called Personify… They don't do any types of like upgrades, any types of like things that better the website… we're as an organization, we're outgrowing what they can provide. And the other thing too is, is they cap us at about 2000 members, at a price point of about roughly 240 to $250 a month."
> — Ken Conly, paddling and social club: Raklet discovery call, February 2026

The complaints heard most often from organizations leaving Wild Apricot:

- **Stagnant product development.** Two notable updates in 27 months (email editor in February 2024; website redesign in July 2025). Feature requests from years ago remain unaddressed on their public backlog.
- **Contact-based pricing creates billing surprises.** Wild Apricot bills by total contacts in the system, including alumni, lapsed members, and prospects. Organizations with historical contact records often pay for a higher tier than their active membership count would suggest.
- **No native refund processing.** Wild Apricot can collect payments but cannot process refunds. Every refund requires a manual step in the external payment processor and a manual record update inside Wild Apricot.
- **Analytics that require CSV exports.** Native reporting shows current totals only. Tracking growth trends, churn, or event attendance over time requires downloading data and working in a spreadsheet.
- **Support quality declined post-acquisition.** Multiple Capterra reviews note a shift to email-only support with longer response times since the Personify acquisition.
- **PE ownership risk.** Momentive Software (TA Associates) acquired Personify in January 2026, putting Wild Apricot under its second PE owner.

## What to Look for in a Wild Apricot Alternative

1. **Pricing transparency and structure.** Contact-based billing is not inherently bad, but understand what counts as a contact on each platform. Flexible add-on packs let you scale contact capacity without being forced into a higher plan tier.
2. **Payment refund capability.** For paid events and annual dues, issuing refunds from inside the membership system saves significant manual overhead.
3. **Ownership and product trajectory.** PE-backed platforms face margin optimization pressure (deferred R&D, support restructuring, pricing adjustments). Independent platforms typically show more consistent product investment.
4. **Analytics and reporting depth.** Membership growth, churn rate, event attendance trends, and renewal rates from inside the platform — or CSV exports?

## The Alternatives

### 1. Raklet — *the page author*
Techstars + Microsoft Ventures alumni · Independent · Founded 2013 · San Francisco (remote team)

Covers Wild Apricot's core functions (member database, event registration, email campaigns, dues collection, member portal) plus three capabilities Wild Apricot lacks: native refund processing, custom white-label iOS and Android apps, and membership growth/churn trend reports without CSV export. Documented REST API. Admitted weakness vs Wild Apricot: no turnkey template website builder — Raklet's page builder is code-based (HTML/CSS/JS).

- **Pricing:** Free plan; paid from $49/mo. Contact-based with add-on contact packs.
- **Best for:** Growing membership orgs needing refunds, branded apps, transparent pricing.

### 2. MemberClicks
PE-owned (Personify/Momentive) · Founded 1998 · 200+ employees

Full AMS with governance, dues management, chapter management, certification tracking, career centers. Same PE ownership family as Wild Apricot.

- **Pricing:** Quote only; typically $5,000–$15,000/yr reported, plus implementation.
- **Best for:** Mid-size professional associations (200+ members) with complex governance needs.

### 3. GrowthZone
PE-backed (Lead Edge Capital, May 2023) · Founded 1996 · 100–200 employees

Targets chambers of commerce and trade associations: member retention analytics, event management, communication tools, GrowthZone Hub member portal.

- **Pricing:** Quote only; typically mid-market.
- **Best for:** Chambers of commerce and trade associations.

### 4. ClubExpress
PE-owned (Lumaverse Technologies, June 2022) · Founded 2002

Most affordable in the comparison for small clubs. Small monthly base fee plus low per-member rate — highly predictable. Member directory, events, email, basic website. Dated interface.

- **Pricing:** ~$22/mo base + ~$0.05–$0.10/member/mo. Transparent public pricing.
- **Best for:** Small clubs (<500 members) on tight budgets needing a directory + event calendar.

### 5. Glue Up
Venture-backed · Founded 2015 · 50–200 employees

Event-driven membership orgs and professional associations, especially Asia/Middle East/Europe. Native ticketing, CRM, community features. Among the strongest event tooling in the category. Steeper onboarding.

- **Pricing:** From ~$125/mo, annual commitment required.
- **Best for:** Event-driven associations running many paid events.

### 6. MemberPlanet
Independent · Founded 2011 · 11–50 employees

Lightweight platform with a free tier — accessible to small volunteer-run orgs. Member management, event registration, email, online dues. Free tier has significant contact/communication limits.

- **Pricing:** Free tier; paid from ~$25/mo.
- **Best for:** Very small orgs (<200 members) or volunteer-run groups.

### 7. Tendenci
Open source · Founded 2001 · 11–50 employees

Only open-source option in the comparison. Self-host free, or managed cloud from ~$125/mo. Full data ownership and customization without vendor permission; requires technical capacity.

- **Pricing:** Free self-hosted; cloud from ~$125/mo.
- **Best for:** Orgs with technical staff wanting open-source flexibility and data control.

### 8. iMIS
PE-owned (Aptify / Community Brands) · Enterprise AMS

Enterprise-grade: full financials, CRM, certifications, deep governance. Deployments take months with consulting partners.

- **Pricing:** Quote only; typical annual contracts well above $10,000.
- **Best for:** Large associations (2,000+ members) with enterprise needs.

## FAQ Highlights

- **Best direct replacement (per Raklet):** Raklet — same core use cases plus refunds, branded mobile apps, flexible pricing. Enterprise needs → MemberClicks or iMIS.
- **Cheapest:** ClubExpress (~$22/mo base). MemberPlanet and Raklet have free tiers. Tendenci free if self-hosted.
- **Migration time from Wild Apricot:** CSV export → field mapping → re-import. Basic member data within hours; event/payment history and custom fields take longer. Orgs report 1–4 weeks total with parallel testing.
- **Wild Apricot ownership timeline:** Personify Corp acquired it September 2017; Momentive Software (TA Associates PE) acquired Personify January 2026. Two notable product updates in the 27 months before April 2026.

## Takeaways for QuiltHosting

The recurring pain points that drive switching — and that QuiltHosting should nail natively:

1. **Transparent, predictable pricing** — no billing surprises from lapsed/alumni contacts counting against tiers. (Ken Conly quote: ~$240–250/mo at a 2,000-member cap was the breaking point.)
2. **Native refund processing** from inside the platform (Wild Apricot's most-cited functional gap).
3. **Built-in trend analytics** — growth, churn, renewal rates, event attendance over time, no CSV exports.
4. **Responsive support and visible product cadence** — the post-acquisition decay is what customers narrate first.
5. **Independence as a selling point** — "not PE-owned" is an explicit evaluation criterion in this market.
